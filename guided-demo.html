BIL Professional Suite · Presentation Edition

Guided Stakeholder Demonstration

A calm, evidence-led walkthrough designed for councils, health, policing, probation, education and central-government conversations.

01

Open with the problem

Practitioners collect large volumes of information, but behavioural change, chronology, uncertainty and decision rationale are often fragmented across systems.

2 minutes
02

Show the professional suite

Demonstrate the three field logs, the Case Workspace, Manager Dashboard and BIL Behaviour Engine. Emphasise one shared method: baseline, change, context, clusters, culture and calibrated conclusion.

4 minutes
03

Create a demonstration case

Use fictional initials only. Add a Green baseline event, an Amber drift event and a Red escalation event. Link BTE codes and show the timeline changing visually.

5 minutes
04

Show decision support

Open Behaviour Summary, Risk and AI Assistant. Explain that outputs create hypotheses, identify missing information and suggest questions; they do not replace professional judgement or statutory thresholds.

5 minutes
05

Generate a professional report

Show the chronology, behavioural summary, BTE/DRS references, protective factors, uncertainties and recommendations in one printable document.

3 minutes
06

Address governance openly

Open Governance & Assurance. State clearly that the current build is presentation and controlled-pilot software, not an approved statutory case-management system.

3 minutes
07

Ask for the next decision

Propose a time-limited, non-live-data discovery pilot with practitioners and managers to test usability, reporting quality, time saved and assurance requirements.

2 minutes

Suggested closing line

“BIL does not ask professionals to surrender judgement to software. It gives them a disciplined way to observe change, separate fact from inference, expose uncertainty and make the next decision more visible. We are seeking a controlled pilot partner to test that proposition safely.”
