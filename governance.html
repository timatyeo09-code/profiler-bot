Governance & Assurance

A transparent statement of the current controlled-pilot prototype controls, professional limitations and the assurance work required before operational public-sector use.

Current control

Local-first records

Field and case records are stored in this browser. They are not automatically shared with BIL or another organisation unless a user exports them or activates a configured cloud integration.

Transparent data flow

AI analysis

AI analysis is not local-only. Text submitted to the Behaviour Engine or case AI tools is sent to the configured Anthropic service through the BIL API. This demonstration build must use fictional, synthetic or appropriately de-identified material.

Current control

Evidence discipline

The interface repeatedly distinguishes observed facts, inference, alternative explanations and unknowns. Outputs state that behaviour is information, not proof.

Required before operations

Identity and access

Government use requires organisational identity, multi-factor authentication, role-based permissions, session management and account lifecycle controls.

Critical requirement

Secure shared storage

A managed UK-region data service, encryption, backups, retention schedules, deletion controls, audit logs and incident response are required before storing live case data.

Required before operations

Data protection

A controller/processor model, DPIA, lawful-basis assessment, records of processing, privacy information, data-sharing arrangements and supplier assurance must be completed.

Required before operations

Accessibility and usability

The product is responsive across phone, tablet, laptop and desktop. Formal WCAG testing, keyboard testing, screen-reader review and user research with practitioners remain required.

Service-specific review

Legal and statutory references

References to Care Act 2014 practice in England and Domestic Abuse Act 2021 practice in England and Wales are workflow prompts, not legal certification. Each adopting service must review current legislation, guidance, thresholds and local procedure before live use.

Operational boundary

This version is a presentation and controlled-pilot prototype. It should not be represented as accredited, government-approved, a statutory case-management system, or suitable as the sole official record. Any live deployment must follow the adopting organisation’s information governance, procurement, safeguarding, security and clinical/digital assurance processes.
