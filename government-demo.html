Behavioural Intelligence Lab · Government Demonstration

Structured observation. Calibrated analysis. Better questions.

BIL Professional Suite is a local-first decision-support prototype that helps practitioners record observable behaviour, separate evidence from inference, identify change over time and produce consistent professional summaries.

Demonstration workflow

1. CreateNon-identifying case reference
2. ObserveRecord facts and context
3. StructureFour States, Five Cs, BTE and DRS
4. AnalyseGenerate calibrated hypotheses
5. ReportProduce a reviewable professional summary

Proposed public-sector value

  • More consistent behavioural recording across teams.
  • Clearer separation between observed fact, professional inference and unknowns.
  • Faster chronology review and identification of behavioural drift.
  • Structured prompts that support supervision and professional curiosity.
  • Reusable reports for controlled review—not automated decisions.

Suggested pilot

  • One service area and a small trained practitioner group.
  • Use synthetic or appropriately governed test cases first.
  • Measure completion time, consistency, usability and supervisory value.
  • Compare reports against existing practice and quality standards.
  • Complete DPIA, security review and accessibility testing before live case use.

What the platform does not do

  • It does not diagnose.
  • It does not prove deception or establish abuse.
  • It does not replace statutory guidance, supervision or professional judgement.
  • It does not make autonomous safeguarding decisions.

Presentation checklist

  • Live demonstration on phone, tablet and desktop.
  • Walk through one synthetic case from observation to report.
  • Show the governance and assurance page.
  • Present the enterprise security roadmap separately from the prototype.
  • Agree measurable pilot outcomes before procurement discussions.
Current status: This build is suitable for demonstration, controlled testing and workflow evaluation. It is not yet an operational government case-management system. Secure shared storage, identity management, permissions, audit logging, retention controls and formal assurance remain required.
