Behavioural Intelligence Lab · Version 4.0.4

BIL Professional Suite

A professional decision-support platform for structured observation, case chronology, behavioural analysis and report generation in high-stakes public-service environments.

The problem

Behavioural information is frequently recorded as narrative, separated from baseline, timing, context, uncertainty and later recovery. Managers must reconstruct patterns manually.

The proposition

BIL creates a consistent workflow: observe, baseline, detect change, cluster evidence, apply context, state uncertainty and identify the next professional action.

Current capabilities

  • Child welfare, adult safeguarding and domestic-abuse field logs
  • Case workspace and visual chronology
  • Searchable BTE library and cautious DRS support
  • Risk, behaviour summary and AI-assisted review
  • Manager dashboard and printable reports
  • Responsive phone, tablet, laptop and desktop access

Intended users

  • Children's and adult services
  • Safeguarding and domestic-abuse teams
  • Police, probation and justice services
  • Health, education and housing practitioners
  • Managers, supervisors and quality-assurance leads

Founder evidence

  • 29 years analysing human behaviour
  • 2,000+ healthcare professionals trained
  • 5,000+ behavioural assessments completed
  • Former Senior Practitioner in Secure Mental Health

Pilot offer

One service area, up to 10 trained practitioners and synthetic data first, with structured feedback and jointly agreed measures.

Measures to test

  • Time saved preparing chronology and summaries
  • Improved fact/inference separation
  • Completeness of evidence and uncertainty recording
  • Practitioner usability across devices
  • Manager confidence in case review

Operational boundary

This presentation edition is not government-approved, accredited or suitable as the sole official record. AI-submitted text is processed by the configured external AI service. Live public-sector deployment requires organisational authentication, secure shared storage, audit logging, retention controls, accessibility testing, DPIA, security assurance, contractual review and service-specific professional safety review.
