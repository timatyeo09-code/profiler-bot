Daily reports

When configured and deployed, the scheduler saves the previous UTC day's summary at 06:00 UTC. Reports are available here; email delivery is not enabled.

Usage starts when collection is enabled; older activity cannot be reconstructed. Tool opens are self-reported and may be missed or repeated. AI requests count accepted requests to /api/profile, not completed reports or clinical outcomes. /api/chat, local case edits and exports are not tracked. Reporting is not a security audit, a measure of staff productivity or a live uptime monitor. No case notes, transcripts, passwords or AI output are collected here.
